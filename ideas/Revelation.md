I had a major revelation this morning regarding the purpose of this book and the internal structures that will reveal the intentions I have for it. It's curious how can start with an idea, hidden deep within memories and the waking mind, and it takes months of writing to excavate how the idea informed the plan you never knew you had. On the train this morning, rolling past the Hawkesbury River, I realised what the protagonist is doing, and how his journey will be unveiled in all the ways I hoped for across the remainder of the book. I needed the first 30 pages of the book written in order to get the foundation for the remaining 2/3 to take shape. Happy days - now, to make it become real with words.
