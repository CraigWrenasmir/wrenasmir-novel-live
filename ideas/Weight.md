# Weight

Part of the idea behind this book is that it should wrestle with the idea of whether the 'weight' of the world has shifted to the virtual, that for most of our history the world was both what we saw of it and the representations we made of it (in our heads, in our art, in our conversations, our dreams, etc).

We are mostly invisible - we have a physical form, but the amount of consciousness in our heads is immense. Yet, the world always felt bigger, like it had more history, more geography, more physicality.

Now, it feels to me that the world is not bigger than the digital brain now holds, that the representation of the world has grown bigger than the world itself, and it is no longer being manifest in our heads as the primary mode of representation, but rather our mind is consuming a massive about of second-hand representative information.

The world is smaller than its representation, and the representation is coming from a space that does not start in our own private subjectivity but rather is given to us by way of the enormous digital brain that now dominates human life.
