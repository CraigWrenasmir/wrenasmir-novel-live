I'm listening to the new EP from Flying Lotus, Big Mama, which is perfect for the subject matter I'm thinking about, because its cartoon maximalism aligns with what's in my head after watching a video with my son from the TV series Adventure Time. It was a video that catalogued an array of characters, and it prompted something within me that I've thought about in the past, about writing a book that contains many cartoon-like characters, fantastically projected onto the daydream consciousness of the protagonist, on the gap between the ontology of the protagonist's lifeworld and the disjecta of their imagination, fuelled by how many hours of Sunday morning TV, comics, advertisements et al. All of which is to say I'm thinking about this element and how it might relate to this book - the bushranger with the jetpack in the vaporwave foodcourt is part of this space, for sure, but I'm not sure how a menagerie of other characters might infiltrate the mind of the book... something to think about as the cartoon ricochets of Big Mama spin up new colours in front of my listening eyes.
