The book is a constellation of six stories with a nested ontology, in that no single story contains the foundation of reality. It seems, at first, that the container is the perspective of a man on a train looking out the window, on his way to see his friend. But this story too contains evidence that it is not the bottom, not the grounding truth. Nothing is privileged as the originating layer. Their Most August Public Organ will appear in multiple guises:

- As a paperback title in the Newcastle couch surfer's world
- As a filename on a solar mesh network
- As a menu item in the vaporwave food court
- As a phrase embedded in dialogue or quoted text
- As a half-forgotten line that resurfaces elsewhere

But these appearances are never highlighted. They flicker past like artefacts in a daydream. The reader senses the recursion without being instructed.
