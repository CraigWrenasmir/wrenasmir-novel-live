SCENE SCAFFOLD

Imagined Node Conversations

Rank: Ten Years of Aftermath Tier: Medium · 1 p Plan §§12, 20 Band: root Condition: UNCERTIFIED

The job — what this scene must accomplish

A hotel room on a distant plain, late afternoon turning to night, and a conversation between two trees forty kilometres apart that may exist nowhere but in the man imagining it. Three loads:

  • Show what ten years have done to his perception. He now instinctively imagines hidden systems speaking through the landscape — not as a decision, but as the default grammar of his attention. The scene must enact the habit, never diagnose it.
  • Stage the node conversation with full sensory conviction and zero certification. Bird recordings exchanged across forty kilometres, rendered so concretely the reader briefly forgets the frame — while the scene never once claims transmission actually occurred.
  • Carry the wonderings about Katita as one open chord: whether she listens, whether she understands them, whether she taught them, whether they taught her, whether she has stopped needing to know, whether the conversation exists only in his imagination. Six notes, no resolution.

The emotional logic underneath (never spoken): the creators built the language, and the nodes now speak it to one another — children talking to each other rather than to their parents. Pride, exclusion, uncertainty, in that order and all at once.

Where it sits

Currently remainder p. 39 (≈ MS p. 91) — third block of the Aftermath rank. The strong sequence: the signal check gave an instrument's non-answer (pp. 36–37), the rumour gave hearsay's rich answer (p. 38), and now imagination — the third and most generous receiver — takes the material and runs. The order can shift, but this block wants the rumour somewhere behind it.

Dialogue permissions

Leif may

Furnish the exchange with technical tenderness — retry schedules, quiet bands, dawn chorus compressed and forwarded — in Katita's engineering manner, without her name attached. Choose the birds at one end and admit he can't name the birds at the other. Fall asleep before the exchange finishes.

The prose may

Let the imagining thicken until the frame recedes — then surface the frame again with the room (the fan, the blind, a road train) rather than with a disclaimer. Give the nodes patience, manners, turn-taking. Let the six wonderings arrive dispersed, as images and postures, one or two clauses each.

No one may

Confirm a signal, or deflate one — no evidence the conversation is real, no "of course it was only in his head." Give the nodes words: they exchange recordings, weather, time — never remarks, never anything about people. Open the pannier. Judge Katita's choice. Say network, archive, emergence, co-author. Philosophy stays silent.

Sensory bank — raw material, take what serves

  • The plain: treeless to the curve of the earth, mirage-line dissolving as the heat lets go, a two-storey hotel visible for twenty minutes of riding before it grows any larger.
  • The room: western light through a holland blind, bricks giving back the day's heat, a ceiling fan with one speed, lace curtain breathing, the pub below going quiet on a weeknight.
  • Sounds: crested pigeons' wing-whistle, galahs working themselves up at dusk, corrugated iron ticking as it cools, a road train passing at long intervals — each one a held breath of pressure against the windows.
  • The imagined geography: one node in a travelling-stock-reserve red gum, one in a homestead windbreak forty kilometres east — the distance held as riding time, not as a number on a map.
  • The imagined exchange: dawn chorus recorded at one end, sent in the cool of night when the band is quiet; a retry after interference; an acknowledgment the length of a wren's note.
  • Beer sweating on the windowsill; moths against the flyscreen; the plain going navy, then black; stars arriving in ones and twos.
  • The blind's pull-cord tapping the sill in the fan's draught — an almost-rhythm the room runs on its own (a hair's-breadth pre-echo of p. 40; one touch, no comment).
  • Bird options he might cast: white-plumed honeyeaters, a butcherbird's morning phrase, corellas in the reserve — and at the far end, something he has never learned the name of.

Traps — the known failure modes

  • Certification, either direction. A flicker at the window, a coincidence of birdsong "answering" — anything that suggests the exchange is really happening breaks the block. Equally fatal: labelling it fantasy. The scene lives in the unmarked middle.
  • Nodes as people. No dialogue attributed to modules, no personalities, no intentions beyond schedule and patience. They exchange recordings, not remarks. The moment a node "wants," the tenderness collapses into whimsy.
  • The question list. Six wonderings typed as six consecutive interrogatives is an essay. Disperse them across the passage as images, postures, half-clauses; two may sit together, never more.
  • The Katita cameo. She must not appear in imagined scene-time — no picture of her kneeling at a node with an ear to the bark. The wonderings gesture at her; the imagery keeps its distance.
  • Diagnosing the habit. "Ten years had taught him to hear systems everywhere" — the sentence the scene exists to avoid. Show the habit misfiring once (a live bird heard as a recording) and trust it.
  • Vocabulary leak. This is the root band at its most exposed: network, mesh, protocol, archive must be rationed to near zero; the engineering arrives as manners, schedules, courtesy to the band.

Ten sparks — optional ways in, steal freely

  1. Open with a live crested pigeon heard as a recording — one beat of miscategorisation before he corrects himself. The habit shown misfiring is the habit proven, without a word of diagnosis.
  2. He gives the far node the courtesy of waiting for full dark before it replies — politeness to the quiet band, an engineering manner borrowed from someone the sentence doesn't name.
  3. The exchange imagined as a mail schedule: dawn collected like letters, held through the heat, sent when the air cools — the postal fantasy of the Boxer years running on new infrastructure. Don't note the rhyme.
  4. He casts the birds honestly: honeyeaters at the near end, and at the far end something he never learned — his imagination admitting its gaps, which is what makes the rest of it credible.
  5. The wonderings arrive as postures, not questions: an ear against bark somewhere; a lit kitchen with the radio off; a woman not needing to check. Half a sentence each, unattributed, dropped.
  6. A road train passes and shakes the room; he folds the interruption into the schedule — the exchange pausing until the noise floor drops — the real world conscripted into the imagined one's etiquette.
  7. The fan's one speed becomes the passage's carrier wave: every imagined transmission rides its rhythm, and when he finally switches it off the silence is enormous and full.
  8. Somewhere in the imagining, details he doesn't remember supplying: a third participant, an unfamiliar pause-length. It stays inside imagination — but his own faculty has begun exceeding his instructions, and he notices the feeling, not the meaning.
  9. He falls asleep mid-exchange and the birds continue without him — the exclusion enacted so gently it reads as lullaby. The children keep talking; the parent sleeps.
  10. End at full dark, window open: the plain silent, or not silent — and let the sentence decline to say which. The refusal is the block's signature.

Exit condition

One page. It ends in the dark — before sleep or just inside it — with nothing confirmed in either direction. The reader should come away having enjoyed the conversation as though it happened while being aware, on reflection, that they were never told it did. If the scene proves the nodes are talking, it has failed; if it dismisses them as fantasy, it has failed identically. The six wonderings must all be present and none answered — and Leif should seem less lonely during the imagining than after it, without the prose saying so.
