THE PRESSURE MAP

The Remainder as an Organ

Fifty pages, post-Page 38, arranged as pipes on a wind-chest. Each block is a pipe: its height is its page budget, its colour is its band, and it fills as you lay pages down. Drag blocks to rearrange the manuscript order — the running page numbers recalculate. Remainder page n ≈ manuscript page n+52.

Click a block's title to show or hide its beats. Click the dots to mark pages written. Rearrange with the arrows (◀ ▶ within a rank, ▲ ▼ between ranks) or by dragging a card.
