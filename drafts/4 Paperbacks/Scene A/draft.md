Were the notation to be seen from above, like sideways music, flat beneath the feet, the notes could well be markers for a shifting of weight left and right like the boxer used to write me in letters during a summer past. Knees slightly bent, light on the ball as you drop to the heel and pivot east and then west and back and repeat east on a hop like a hi hat paradiddle. The boxer used to write me although I had no fixed address, nor did he for that matter. I'd get a sense of a pub on the regional plains that he was moving towards and I'd write the letter in that direction, I suppose. So too he'd intimate that I was sleeping on one or another couch of one or another girl who had taken me in over the summer, if not on a golf course or beachhead or rooftop during the balmy nights, of which they all were other than when loneliness dropped the degrees and sideways music could be heard all at once from one or another balcony before, during and after, forever and ever, or so it seemed. I read so many paperbacks from the pulp of Australia's twentieth century output that summer, of which I transcribed paragraphs for the boxer.
